About this site

Fairwave nodes form a mesh and default to local breakout: your traffic exits at this site rather than a distant hub. The network is provided as-is for community use. Spectrum transmission is gated by operator authorization and applicable law. Fairwave project

Privacy notice. This hotspot observes standard Wi-Fi operator practices: connection logs (time, MAC hash) are kept briefly for abuse prevention, contain no subscriber credentials, and are never sold. SIM material on this network is provisioned under strict vault rules; see the Fairwave privacy policy for details.
